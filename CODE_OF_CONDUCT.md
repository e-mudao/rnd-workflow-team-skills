# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, national origin, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectfull and constructive in issues, PRs and reviews.
- Critique ideas and artifacts, not people. Locate findings to files and evidence.
- Accept that reviewers keep the team-edition boundaries: evidence over assertions, and member completion is not integrated completion.
- Do not derail a thread; take side topics to a new issue.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination in any form.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, PRs, reviews, commit messages, and any place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a participant. Reports go to the maintainers through a private channel (for example GitHub's private report tools) rather than a public thread.

This code is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, and is released under the same terms as this project.
